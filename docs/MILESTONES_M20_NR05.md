## How to read the evidence

[Historical results](test-evidence-m20-nr05/HISTORICAL_RESULTS.json) are attributed
reports, not reproduced native tests. [New logs](test-evidence-m20-nr05/EVIDENCE_INDEX.md)
record execution of the included public references. The nine reference checks
are intentionally much narrower than the original milestone corpora.

Later historical results do not erase failed attempts. In particular, the M29
build failures, M35 preparation failure, M41 first compilation failure and NR03
precompiler stop remain distinct from their corrected runs. Later documentation
reusing the 1,194-test workspace baseline does not create additional passes.

The main research progression is from content and traffic-observation limits,
through explicit resource-model assumptions and counterexamples, to conditional
consensus mechanics and a sampled-batch ideal-oracle resource bound. None of
those stages alone establishes operational useful-resource consensus.
