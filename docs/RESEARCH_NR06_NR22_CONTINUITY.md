# Research continuity from NR06 through NR22

**Scope:** selected historical research chronology, October-November 2026.
This is a high-level record for continuity with the previously released
M20-M41/NR01-NR05 research. It is not the complete original Rust code,
runtime logs, original proof material, or an independent re-execution.
Individual claims remain limited to their research models and assumptions.

## Continuation and negative findings

- **NR06-NR09:** native visible-batch resource observations, regeneration and
  cache-cost comparisons, additional native sparse-response checks, and
  recorded restrictions on the earlier accepted-proof/resource bridge.
- **NR10-NR13:** continued resource-consensus research, including the limits
  of conditional native-resource admission; previous bounds were not promoted
  to unconditional production guarantees.
- **NR14:** cross-architecture task-composition research retained negative
  results about coalition resource attribution.
- **NR15:** native useful-traffic task experiments examined two-epoch
  attribution counterexamples.
- **NR16:** simultaneous useful-traffic and charged-history research retained
  negative results and a bounded handoff rather than asserting completion.
- **NR17:** independent-receiver and Sybil-selection boundaries examined
  funded transport and participant-independence assumptions.
- **NR18:** coalition admission research preserved verifiable finite
  counterexamples.
- **NR19:** two-registration delivery tests examined whether colluding
  endpoints can produce apparently valid public evidence.
- **NR20:** receiver-cut admission feedback maintained conditional bounds.
- **NR21:** resource-ledger conservation, origin/custody accounting and
  transition invariants were revised and checked; necessary accounting
  conditions are not a physical-scarcity proof.
- **NR22:** root-event soundness identified the external witness or
  independently verifiable cost anchor needed to move beyond evidence
  controlled by cooperating endpoints.

The NR22 boundary motivated an independent storage-power construction,
recorded as NE-01. The NE-02 S1 adversarial baseline then demonstrated that
NE-01's initial seal still lacked an enforceable storage/time margin.

The chronology is deliberately compressed; stages NR06-NR13 are grouped because
no complete independently reproducible source bundle for those stages is
supplied here. The exact historical results may carry stronger restrictions.
No claim of full production consensus, native token launch, or root-resource
security follows from this narrative.
